# Hospital Quality & Operations Dashboard (Donabedian Framework)

🔗 **[View Live Interactive Dashboard](https://rachna07m.github.io/hospital-quality-dashboard/)**

## Executive Summary
Hospital administrators and clinical directors often evaluate departmental performance using isolated volume or financial metrics, missing the underlying operational bottlenecks that drive adverse clinical outcomes. This interactive dashboard applies **Avedis Donabedian’s Quality Framework** to hospital operations data, linking structural capacity and clinical workflow efficiency directly to patient safety and cost control.

---

## Analytical Framework: Structure, Process, Outcome

| Dimension | Operational & Clinical Focus | Key Metrics Tracked |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Structure** *(Inputs & Capacity)* | Evaluates physical infrastructure, bed allocation, staffing ratios, and departmental resource readiness. | Bed Occupancy Rate, Staff-to-Patient Ratio, Departmental Capacity Utilization |
| **Process** *(Workflow & Execution)* | Measures clinical throughput, scheduling adherence, and delays across the patient care continuum. | Pre-Treatment Wait Time (Mins), Average Length of Stay (ALOS), Turnaround Variance |
| **Outcome** *(Clinical & Financial Impact)* | Tracks patient safety benchmarks, care quality, and financial efficiency across acuity tiers. | 30-Day Readmission Rate, Complication / Infection Rate, Cost per Case (Acuity-Adjusted) |

---

## Clinical & Management Highlights
* **Acuity-Adjusted Benchmarking:** Stratifies departmental performance by patient severity levels to distinguish genuine operational bottlenecks from expected clinical complexity.
* **Bottleneck Root-Cause Isolation:** Connects upstream process delays (such as extended wait times and slow bed turnover) to downstream increases in Length of Stay (LOS) and readmission risk.
* **Executive Usability:** Built with interactive department filters, dynamic KPI cards, and responsive viewing modes for rapid decision-making during clinical governance reviews.
